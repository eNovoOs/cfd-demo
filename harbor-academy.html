What a day looks like

Circle time, letters, colors and friends. The support is built into the room, so the day still looks like preschool.

  1. Arrival and settling in

    A calm start, with sensory supports from the moment a child walks in.

  2. Connection first

    Adults follow the child's lead in play, then build early learning on top of that connection.

  3. Play-based learning

    Letters, colors, numbers and early skills, taught through play rather than at a table.

  4. Small-group play

    A few children at a time, with an adult close by so each child gets a turn and the support to take it.

  5. Sensory supports throughout

    Movement, quiet spaces and tools that help a child settle are part of the whole day, not a separate slot.

  6. Structured transitions

    Clear, predictable handoffs between activities and at pickup.

Who Harbor Academy is built for

Families come to us when a big classroom is not working. Choosing a smaller room is not admitting something is wrong. It is choosing the room where a child can do well.

A child who needs a smaller room

Big classrooms move fast and get loud. Some children do better with fewer people, a slower pace and an adult close by.

With or without a diagnosis

No diagnosis is needed to enroll. Some families come with one, many do not, and some are not looking for one. Harbor Academy is preschool either way.

Questions parents ask first

Does my child need a diagnosis?

No. Harbor Academy does not require a diagnosis. Families come with one, without one, and some are not looking for one.

Is this instead of preschool?

Harbor Academy is preschool. A small, play-based classroom for ages 3 to 6, with more support built into the room.

Do you take insurance?

Harbor Academy is private pay: $2,950 a month, or $3,700 with extended hours. Because there is no insurance approval to wait on, a child can usually start quickly.

What are the hours?

Core day hours: TBC. Extended hours: TBC.

Why is it this expensive?

A small classroom, therapeutic staff and a room built for this. It is a different cost structure from a standard preschool.

How is this different from the DIR/Floortime Day Program?

The day program is for children ages 3 to 6 with an autism diagnosis. It is a therapeutic day, billed through out-of-network benefits or Medicaid. Harbor Academy is preschool, private pay, and no diagnosis is needed.

What if my child also needs therapy?

Individual therapies, including occupational, feeding and aquatic therapy, are offered at the same center for ages birth to 21 and are billed separately as out-of-network services.
