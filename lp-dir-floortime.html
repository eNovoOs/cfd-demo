What a day looks like

A day program, not a series of appointments. Our daily routine provides the predictability children need, with intentional flexibility to follow each child's lead, interests, and developmental needs.

  1. Arrival and settling in

    Sensory supports are part of the whole day, starting at drop-off.

  2. Morning DIR/Floortime play

    Play led by the child's interests, which is where connection and communication grow.

  3. Small-group social time

    1:1 adult support to help children navigate peer interactions and build social confidence.

  4. Play-based learning

    Learning through play, with a focus on building the developmental foundations for connection, communication, and thinking.

  5. Intentional food exploration

    Low-pressure opportunities to explore new foods while building comfort, curiosity, and confidence around eating.

  6. Swimming, two days a week

    Water is part of the therapy, not a break from it.

  7. Structured transitions

    Clear, calm handoffs between activities and at pickup.

Regulation first. Skills are built on top of that.

DIR/Floortime is a developmental, relationship-based approach. Growth happens as each capacity builds on one another.

CAPACITY 1

Regulation

Feeling calm and safe enough to engage.

CAPACITY 2

Connection

Sharing attention and enjoyment with a trusted adult.

CAPACITY 3

Communication

Back-and-forth exchanges, with or without words.

CAPACITY 4

Problem-solving

Working through a goal together, step by step.

CAPACITY 5

Flexibility

Handling change and new ideas with more ease.

Questions parents ask first

Do you take my insurance?

We are out of network. We verify your benefits before services begin and give you a clear breakdown of what your plan will reimburse. Medicaid is accepted for DIR/Floortime services.

How long until my child can start?

Benefits verification and an evaluation usually take two weeks to a month.

Does my child need a diagnosis?

Yes, the day program is for children ages 3 to 6 with an autism diagnosis.

Is this instead of preschool?

Yes. It is a full day that replaces a traditional preschool placement.

We already tried therapy and it did not work.

That is useful information, not a failure. It usually tells us where to start.
