What is different here

The same approach runs through the preschool, the day program and every single session.

Regulation first

Feeling calm and safe comes before every skill. That order does not change, whichever room a child is in.

Relationship-based, by design

DIR/Floortime is a developmental approach that follows the child's interests. Adults join the play, and communication grows from that connection.

Founder-led, on the floor

Courtney St. Germain, OTR/L and DIR-Expert practitioner, founded the practice and works on the floor with the team.

Plain about billing

No in-network contracts. We verify your out-of-network benefits before anything starts, and Harbor Academy is private pay with no approval to wait on.

Children learn best when they feel safe, connected and understood.
