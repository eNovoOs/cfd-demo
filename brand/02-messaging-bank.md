## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Is this covered by insurance?" | We are out of network. We verify your benefits before services begin and give you a clear breakdown of what your plan will reimburse. Some families qualify for a single case agreement and we pursue those. |
| "How long until we can start?" | For Harbor Academy, quickly, because it is private pay. For the day programme, benefits verification and an evaluation usually take two weeks to a month. |
| "Is this instead of school?" | Harbor Academy is preschool. The day programme is a full day that replaces a traditional preschool placement. |
| "Does my child need a diagnosis?" | Not for Harbor Academy. The day programme does require an autism diagnosis. |
| "We already tried therapy and it did not work." | That is useful information, not a failure. It usually tells us where to start. |
| "Why is it this expensive?" | Three children to one teacher, therapeutic staff, and a room built for this. It is a different cost structure from a standard preschool. |

## Banned words

cure · fix · normal · high functioning · low functioning · suffers from · afflicted · miracle · guaranteed · proven to · breakthrough · transform your child · special needs preschool · leverage · synergy · passionate

## Preferred vocabulary

| Use | Not |
|---|---|
| Autistic child | Child with autism, suffering from autism |
| Sensory needs, sensory differences | Sensory issues, sensory problems |
| Needs more support | Low functioning, severe, challenging |
| Regulation | Behaviour management, compliance |
| Family, parent, caregiver | Client, consumer, case |
| Progress, growth | Outcomes, gains, results |
| A different approach | A better approach than ABA |
