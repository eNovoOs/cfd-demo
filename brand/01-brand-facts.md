### Chart colour (do not substitute brand colours)

Categorical, fixed order, never cycled: `#3F529A`, `#80AE5E`, `#00B9DE`, `#DD7D93`
Sequential, light to dark: `#8DA1E4`, `#6F85CE`, `#566BBA`, `#3E52A7`, `#2D3D92`

Every series also carries a text label: slots two, three and four sit below 3:1 against white (2.6, 2.3, 2.8), so colour alone never identifies a series. Text stays navy, Ink or Slate, never the series colour.

## Typography

Two families only. Both free on Google Fonts.

- **Forum** Regular. Display sizes only: tagline, pull quotes, section openers.
- **Montserrat** 300 to 800. Everything else.

The live site currently also loads Brandon Grotesque, Avenir and Poppins. Those three should be removed.

Scale: Display Forum 56/1.05 · H1 Montserrat 600 34/1.15 · H2 600 21/1.25 · Eyebrow 800 11/1.2 +16% tracking uppercase · Body 400 16/1.65 · Caption 500 12/1.5 Slate. (The eyebrow spec is 800 weight and +16% tracking everywhere; ignore any older 700 / +18% version.)

Body copy never below 16px on screen. Line length 60 to 75 characters. Sentence case headlines with manual line breaks.

## Logo

Primary lockup is the default. Minimum width 180px on screen, 45mm in print. Clear space on all sides equals the height of the tallest circle in the mark.

On navy, on photos, or on any field darker than Slate, the lockup reverses to solid white line art. The colour mark must never sit on navy because its outlines are navy and disappear.

Mark alone is for avatars, favicons and app icons only, where the account name supplies the words. Never the sole identifier in an ad.

Never recolour, stretch, rotate, add effects, rebuild the wordmark in another typeface, or lock the tagline into the lockup.

## Programmes

### Harbor Academy at CFD
Developmental micro-preschool, ages 3 to 6. Ratio 1:3 (client-stated at onboarding; not published on the site, which says only "low student-to-educator ratios"). Play-based, DIR-informed, sensory-friendly. Positioning line: "More Support. Still Preschool." Site tagline: "Safe. Connected. Curious. Growing." Principle: "Connection Before Compliance."
Private pay: $2,950/month core, +$750/month extended hours, $3,700 combined.
Affiliated swim programme with Five Star Swim School, Oakland NJ.

### DIR/Floortime Day Program
Ages 3 to 6, autism diagnosis required. 9:00 to 2:30 Monday to Friday. 12-month programme, open enrolment.
Includes morning DIR/Floortime play, small-group social engagement, play-based learning, sensory supports throughout the day, swimming 3 days a week, structured transitions, therapeutic integration, parent communication and coaching.
Progression taught as: Regulation, Connection, Communication, Problem-solving, Flexibility.
Positioned as "A Therapeutic Alternative to Traditional Preschool or ABA."
Billing: out-of-network benefits or Medicaid. Benefits verified before enrolment; prior authorisation where the plan requires it.
Non-local options: 2-week intensives, 1-month intensives, remote parent training intensives.

### Individual therapies
Birth to 21, any diagnosis. DIR/Floortime, Occupational Therapy, Feeding Therapy (sensory-based), Aquatic Therapy. Out-of-network only.

### The Playful Pathways Project
Proposed, as yet unfunded fMRI neuroimaging study of how relationship-based therapy affects the developing brain, children aged 3 to 10 enrolled in DIR/Floortime at CFD. The site names potential partnerships with Rutgers' Center for Advanced Human Brain Imaging Research, Vanderbilt University, or local imaging centres. Nothing has been scanned and no partnership is confirmed. Phase 1 seeks $30,000 to $50,000 to scan 10 participants at $3,000 to $5,000 each. Sponsorship tier "Neural Explorer" at $3,000 or less funds one child's scans.
Describe as research in progress. Never as evidence already in hand.

## Insurance reality

No in-network contracts. Out-of-network benefits, or Medicaid for DIR/Floortime services. Reimbursement via a monthly superbill the family submits, or claims handled by the practice with credits applied to the account. Single Case Agreements are offered but approval is not guaranteed. Onboarding takes 2 weeks to 1 month and evaluations must be completed to enrol for anyone insurance-based.
