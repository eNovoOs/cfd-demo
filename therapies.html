Four therapies. One approach.

Every session starts the same way: with a child who feels calm and safe enough to engage. The skills come after that, not before.

Occupational therapy

Support for sensory, motor and regulation needs, and the everyday routines they affect. Led by occupational therapy staff.

DIR/Floortime sessions

Relationship-based sessions that follow the child's interests, led by DIR practitioners. Connection first, then communication and problem-solving grow from it.

Feeding therapy

Sensory-based feeding therapy that starts with regulation, not with the plate. Mealtimes are worked on the way the rest of the day is: at the child's pace.

Aquatic therapy

For children who settle in water before they settle anywhere else. Therapy goals worked on in the pool, with the same relationship-based approach as on land.

Coming from outside the area

For families who are not local, and for parents who want coaching without traveling at all. We plan the schedule with you before you book anything.

  1. Two-week intensive

    A concentrated block of sessions at the Oakland center, for families traveling in for a short stay.

  2. One-month intensive

    A longer block, for families who can stay in the area and want more time with the team.

  3. Remote parent training

    Coaching sessions online, wherever you are, for the parent who wants to carry the approach into everyday routines at home.

We are out of network. Here is how that works.

We do not hold in-network contracts. We verify your out-of-network benefits before anything starts and give you a clear breakdown of what your plan reimburses. From there, families choose one of three routes.

1

Monthly superbill

We give you an itemized statement each month. You submit it to your plan and are reimbursed directly.

2

Claims handled by us

We submit the claims for you, and reimbursements are applied as credits to your account.

3

Single case agreement

Some families qualify. We pursue those with your plan, though approval is up to the insurer.
